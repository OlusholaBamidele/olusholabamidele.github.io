Introduction

Hey, I'm Mo! A quick rundown on who I am and what brought me here:

  • My Background:

    I studied Sociology and International Business, and these days I'm working on pivoting into IT and technology.

  • A Little About Me:

    I have a creative and entrepreneurial streak - nail art and content creation are big passions of mine, and I love turning ideas into actual designs. Business, branding, and e-commerce interest me too; I like taking something from a rough concept to a finished product people can actually use.

  • Future Goals:

    My ultimate goal is entrepreneurship. I want to build businesses that allow me to combine my creativity, business knowledge, and growing technology skills. I already enjoy taking ideas and turning them into something tangible, whether that means building a brand, creating a product, or finding a better way to solve a problem.

    As I grow, I want to become a serial entrepreneur, building and scaling businesses across different industries rather than limiting myself to one path. For me, learning technology is another tool that gives me the ability to create, innovate, automate, and bring more of my ideas to life.

    Ultimately, I want to build things that are profitable, sustainable, and genuinely useful, while creating the freedom to keep exploring new ideas and opportunities.

  • My Coding Experience and Why I Chose WEB115:

    I have some basic web design experience from a previous course, where I learned some of the fundamentals of creating and designing websites. Although I still have a lot to learn, that experience gave me a good starting point and made me more comfortable with web development.

    I am taking WEB115 because it is a required course for my program, but I also see it as an opportunity to strengthen my existing skills, learn new techniques, and become more confident in creating professional and functional websites.

  • This Semester's Courses:
    1. CIS110 - Introduction to Computers
    2. CSC151 - JAVA Programming
    3. DBA120 - Database Programming
    4. WEB115 - Web Markup and Scripting
  • When I'm Not Studying:

    I'm a naturally curious person - crime documentaries and random internet rabbit holes are basically my hobby. I'll go looking for one thing online and end up learning about five unrelated topics instead.
